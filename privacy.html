Peptides Nepal

Privacy policy

Short version: we don't collect personal data. No accounts, no tracking, no ads.

Last updated: 29 September 2026. This policy covers the Peptides Nepal website (peptidesnepal.com).

What the app stores

The app keeps a few things on your own device only: the peptides you save, your Myth-or-fact score, whether you have read the opening notice, and a copy of the guide and posts so the app works offline. None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites. We don't sell or share data, because we don't have any.

Network requests

To show the guide and posts, the app downloads files from our verified servers and post images from image servers. Like any web request, these servers see your IP address and device type in their normal logs. We don't use these logs to identify anyone.

Links to Instagram, PubMed, DailyMed and other sources open in your browser or the relevant app. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Contacting us

If you email us or send an Instagram message, we use your message only to reply. We don't add you to any list.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
